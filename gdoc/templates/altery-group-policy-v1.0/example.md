# Purpose

The Altery Group ("Altery" or "Group") refers to all the legal entities
operating under the NFTS Limited umbrella. This Policy sets out how Altery
selects, approves and monitors third-party service providers.

The document is divided into 3 sections:

1. Main body of the document, describing the Policy as it applies to all Group
   entities.
2. Appendices, describing in greater detail the areas in scope of the Policy.
3. Addenda, describing how the Policy is modified for a specific legal entity.

*Precedence:* in case of conflict between the main section, its appendices and
the addenda, the **addenda take precedence** for the entity they name.

## Policy Owner

The Head of Procurement owns this Policy. The Group Chief Risk Officer reviews
its effectiveness annually.

### Delegation

Day-to-day delegation is recorded in the outsourcing register.

# Scope

This Policy applies to all individuals performing work for or on behalf of
Altery or any Group entity, in any jurisdiction. This includes:

- All employees, whether full-time, part-time, temporary or fixed-term
- Contractors, consultants, secondees, interns and volunteers
  - Including agency staff placed through a framework supplier
  - Including individuals on work experience placements
- Executive and Non-Executive members of the Board
- Any third-party or outsourced service provider engaged by the Group

All such individuals must comply with this Policy in the performance of their
duties.

## Out of Scope

Intra-group services between Altery entities are out of scope, unless the
receiving entity is regulated in a jurisdiction that treats them as *material
outsourcing*.

# Supplier Risk Tiering

Suppliers are tiered on inherent risk. The tier drives due diligence depth and
review frequency.

| Tier | Criticality | Due diligence | Review frequency |
|------|-------------|---------------|------------------|
| Tier 1 | Critical or important function | Full on-site assessment | Annually |
| Tier 2 | Material, not critical | Desktop assessment | Every 2 years |
| Tier 3 | Non-material | Questionnaire only | Every 3 years |
| Tier 4 | De minimis spend | Register entry only | On renewal |

The steps to tier a supplier are:

1. Record the service in the outsourcing register.
2. Score inherent risk against the criteria below.
   a. Data sensitivity
   b. Substitutability
   c. Regulatory classification
3. Confirm the tier with the Group Chief Risk Officer.

# Policy Review

This Policy will be reviewed annually and must be approved by the relevant
appointed board level committee, as described in the Group Corporate
Governance Policy.

This Policy will also be reviewed on an ad-hoc basis when:

- There is a change in Altery's risk appetite
- Altery introduces new products or services
- New jurisdictions and markets are introduced
- A major or critical risk event has occurred
