2. The pointer rests on a row

Half a second after the pointer stops on a title or its second line, a detail card drops under the row, inside the panel. Three lines, each a fact the row already shows cut short: the whole name, the last action with its full date and time, the whole note path. It goes the moment the pointer leaves the row, moves onto an icon, or presses anything. A click on the row opens the document in Google Docs.

The card covers the row under it, the way an icon's tooltip already does; moving the pointer down takes the card away and the next row is there. The last grey line, "Click opens in Google Docs", is shown until the first click on any row, then never again, the way the four labels under the icons work today. Not on the card: the link (copy link gives it), who edited, counts of anything. The card is the row uncut, never the row plus. The last row shows the one mark that stays: a run that failed.

3. Right-click, hold, and the icons

Nothing else moves. A right-click or a half-second hold on the title still opens the system menu with Hide, and a press takes the card away first, so the menu and the card are never up together. The four icons keep their own clicks and their own tooltips; while the pointer is over an icon the card is not shown. On the first open after the update the four labels under the first row name the icons.
